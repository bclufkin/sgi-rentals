Key Findings

  • Table covers both July and August 2027 — toggle Month to switch prices and specific date ranges. Where confirmed, "August" shows the earliest available week; "July" shows the latest available week before August.
  • "Beach position": Ocean Front = first row on the beach; Beach View = correct side of road, a house or two back; Second Row = not beachfront.
  • Minutes-to-lighthouse are rough estimates by island zone, not precise addresses — Plantation/East End ~12 min, Central Beach ~3 min.
  • Only Collins publishes a seasonal rate range; most other companies hide pricing behind a live quote tool (marked TBD).
